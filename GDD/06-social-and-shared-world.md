# 06 — Social & Shared World

> **Status: Outline.** Structure and intent fixed; detail to be expanded.

## 6.1 Intent
The town is **shared**: many players inhabit one persistent, server-authoritative simulation.
Interaction is **social, not adversarial** — the multiplayer layer amplifies the life-sim,
it does not turn it into a competitive game.

## 6.2 What exists today (V5)
- **Presence** — players see each other in the world.
- **Chat & emotes** — text communication.
- **Friendships**, **blocks** and **reports** — the social graph with safety controls.
- **Consensual player partnerships** — two players' genoses can partner.
- **Venues** — places that exist for social gathering.
- **Moderation** — moderation notices and tooling.

## 6.3 Design boundaries
- Players never control another player's genos.
- Partnerships are **consensual** and modelled as tyed relationships, not an overlord claim.
- The society simulation runs identically whether zero or many players are online.
