Starting a life is an intent like any other: the server validates it (slot free, household accepting births, no trait budget overspend) and only then does the genos exist.
M09 — A new life. Creation is framed as entering an existing society, not building an avatar: you pick how much continuity you want (born into a family, or an immigrant with no ties), traits are inherited first and nudged with a small budget, and the appearance is derived. This is the screen that makes the genetics pillar honest — and the one that stops "creation" from being a character editor that the simulation ignores.
