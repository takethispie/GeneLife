M06 — People & conversation. One list for players and residents, because the whole premise is that other people's lives are as real as yours; the chip marks who is a person behind the keyboard, not who matters. Chat is diegetic (it happens because Wren is at your door) and a conversation can produce intents — an introduction, a loan, a partnership — rather than being a separate chat app bolted onto a game.
