The Sims' household, but inside a society: who lives here, who earns, who is a ward, what the week actually costs, and what happens to the family when the player's genos dies. Lineage is a recorded document (faded, not hidden, for the dead) and the heir is chosen here — the continuity rule from §5.5 has a home in the UI rather than a dialog on death.
