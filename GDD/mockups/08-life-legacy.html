Why this is not "game over". The personal pillar (Sims) and the genetic pillar (Dwarf-Fortress depth) meet at inheritance: a lineage can be played for generations, and death is a chapter of the town's record, not a reload.
M08 — Life & legacy. A life presented as a record — dates, causes, consequences — because that is exactly what the simulation stores and what makes depth readable. The right page answers the two questions a persistent-life sim must answer honestly: what will the simulation do with me, and what happens to my household when I die. Heir selection lives here, permanently, not in a modal at the moment of death.
