M04 — Work & career. Not a list of jobs, but a gate map: what the ladder needs, what you already meet, and what the apply button can and cannot promise. Pay is broken out to net on the spot (shift pay − tax − pension) because the simulation's money is integer cents and every transfer is ledgered — the player should be able to reconcile their own wage.
