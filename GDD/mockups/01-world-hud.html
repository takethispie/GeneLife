M01 — World view. The play surface. Chrome is warm paper so it stays legible over a saturated tile map; the town is never dimmed by the interface. Top-left is the player (person-first), top-centre is town time (diegetic), top-right is a single digest rather than per-event pop-ups. The bottom day ribbon answers "what will happen today" and is also the schedule editor. The intent dial (right-click on the genos) lists verbs grouped by where the genos is; each verb shows cost and duration before it is chosen and maps 1:1 to a typed intent in the contract.
