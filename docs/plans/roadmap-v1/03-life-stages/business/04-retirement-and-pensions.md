# Retirement and pensions

Retirement is where the economy pays back a lifetime of work. It is also where bad policy becomes visible: an unfunded pension promise collapses the elderly population.

## The transition

At 65 a worker:

- stops working and loses wage income;
- gives up the job, freeing a seat;
- becomes eligible for a pension;
- keeps their housing, but income drops;
- changes consumption and saving behaviour.

The transition must be atomic. A retiree must never remain an employee or a job seeker.

## How the pension works

V1 uses a funded occupational scheme:

- during work, the employee and employer contribute a percentage of gross wages;
- contributions are pooled in a pension fund;
- each worker has a statement recording contributions and contributing cycles;
- at retirement, the statement is converted into a monthly pension;
- payouts come from the fund, not from thin air.

If the fund cannot cover payouts:

1. the government tops it up from tax revenue;
2. if the government cannot, payouts are reduced proportionally;
3. the shortfall is visible and persistent.

There is no hidden money creation.

## Retirement life

Retirees:

- receive a pension monthly;
- continue to pay rent, utilities and food;
- draw on savings when the pension is insufficient;
- may downsize or move to a cheaper unit;
- spend less on discretionary categories;
- have more leisure time and different needs patterns.

A retiree with a paid-off home and savings is comfortable. A retiree who rented all their life and saved little is at risk. That contrast is the point.

## Why retirement matters for the simulation

- It creates a non-working population that still consumes.
- It tests whether wages were high enough to fund a life.
- It creates demand for housing downsizing and care.
- It produces mortality and inheritance, which redistribute wealth.

## Admin controls

- inspect the pension fund balance and funding ratio;
- inspect individual pension statements;
- adjust contribution and payout rates;
- trigger retirement for a sim;
- top up the fund manually for scenario testing;
- see the share of retirees below the poverty line.

## Out of scope

Private pensions, annuities markets, early retirement, phased retirement, and pension investment returns beyond a simple configured yield.
