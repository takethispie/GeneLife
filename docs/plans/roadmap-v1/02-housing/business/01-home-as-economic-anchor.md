# The home as economic anchor

Wages only matter if something consumes them. Housing is the largest recurring cost in an NPC's life and the main reason a job loss becomes urgent.

## What a home provides

- **Safety** — sleeping indoors restores energy; sleeping rough does not.
- **Hygiene** — running water and a bathroom.
- **Stability** — a fixed address for the household and a buffer against bad months.
- **Status** — quality tier affects how well needs recover and how health declines with age.

## Why housing is not just another need

Needs are short-term: hunger rises in hours, sleep in a day. Rent is monthly and contractual. It creates a multi-cycle obligation that savings must absorb. That is what makes a household plan ahead, and what makes unemployment and old age dangerous.

## The household

Rent is paid by a household, not by an individual:

- adults pool income for rent and food;
- children and students are dependents, not separate rent payers;
- a retiree living with family is protected; one living alone is exposed;
- a death can change the household's income overnight.

This is why housing depends on households and why households sit between the individual and the economy.

## The affordability question

Every month a household asks: can we cover rent, food and utilities? The answer depends on:

- current cash and savings;
- wage or pension income;
- rent level for the unit;
- number of earners in the household;
- whether the dwelling is owned outright.

When the answer is no, the household does not instantly become homeless. It first cuts discretionary spending, then dips into savings, then falls into arrears. Eviction is the end of a ladder, not the first step.

## What the admin should be able to see

- rent roll by property and household;
- rent burden as a share of household income;
- arrears ageing;
- vacancy and waiting lists;
- homeless households and shelter occupancy;
- the effect of housing quality on health and needs.

Housing is the clearest visible link between the economy and the population. If it is opaque, the simulation feels arbitrary.
