# Family continuity for players

V4 gave NPC families real parent, partner and guardian edges, inherited traits, births and inheritance. A player family should use the same facts, not a parallel player-only family tree.

## What continuity means for a player

- **A partner is a real relationship.** It creates mirrored partner edges and follows the same eligibility, consent and exclusivity rules as an NPC partnership.
- **A child is a real sim.** It is born through the existing pregnancy and birth path, inherits traits, gets parent edges and joins the household under the same capacity rules.
- **A home is shared.** Household membership, rent, arrears and eviction apply to the player's household, not just the player sim.
- **Death is not a reset.** The estate is settled, debts are paid and an eligible heir can continue. The family keeps its history.
- **Traits matter.** A player's children resemble them because they inherit the same trait schema that drives NPC families and, in V5, appearance.

## Consent and agency

Player–NPC family actions need a deterministic NPC consent policy. Repeated requests must not wear an NPC down, and a player cannot create a partnership or pregnancy by editing a flag. Player–player family actions require an invitation and an explicit acceptance.

## What the player should not get

- A free second household.
- A child without a parent edge or household membership.
- An inheritance that bypasses the estate waterfall.
- A partner who can be created or discarded without an event.
- A family tree that exists only on the client.

## Success signal

A player can die after raising a child, and the child continues in the same house, with the same family edges, the same inherited traits and a settled estate. The admin can inspect the whole chain without a special player-only code path.
