# Resource constraints without punishing players

Utilities and food are necessities. A simulation that makes them impossible to afford is not realistic; it is just cruel. V5 adds constraint without removing the safety nets that already exist.

## Principles

- **Constraint is visible before it is fatal.** Arrears, warnings and service levels appear before an outage or eviction.
- **There is always a fallback.** A household can move to a cheaper unit, use a shelter, buy cheaper food or seek support.
- **No hidden death spiral.** A missed bill does not silently destroy health; it produces a service cut-off with a known effect and a recovery path.
- **Player and NPC rules are the same.** A player household is not exempt, and an NPC household is not a resource sink.
- **The economy stays closed.** Bills and groceries are transfers, not money sinks that vanish without a ledger entry.
- **No pay-to-win.** There is no cash shop, no premium utility and no purchased immunity.

## Failure ladder

1. Bill due.
2. Wallet/savings waterfall pays it.
3. Partial payment becomes arrears.
4. Service level drops; a warning is visible.
5. Service is cut off; needs and health are affected.
6. The household can pay arrears, move or seek support.
7. Eviction remains a separate housing consequence.

## Grocery ladder

1. Household buys food at a store.
2. Food enters the pantry and can spoil.
3. A household that cannot afford food buys cheaper items or uses support.
4. Poor nutrition affects health over time, not instantly.
5. A player can always see why a need is falling.

## What to avoid

- A utility bill that is impossible to predict.
- An outage with no visible cause.
- Food that spoils before it can be eaten with no warning.
- A health penalty that cannot be reversed.
- A player-only exemption that breaks the shared economy.

## Success signal

A household can recover from a missed bill or an empty pantry through play, and the player can explain what went wrong without reading the source.
