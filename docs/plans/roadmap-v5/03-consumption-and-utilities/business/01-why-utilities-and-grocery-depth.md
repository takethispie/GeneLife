# Why utilities and grocery depth

A household budget is only meaningful if the household has real recurring costs and real choices. Today utilities are one abstract number and grocery is a minimal spend. The player pays rent, but the home does not consume anything specific and the shop does not run out of anything.

V5 gives consumption a shape.

## What utilities add

- **A service, not a fee.** Water and electricity have production, capacity and load. A growing town can outgrow its supply.
- **Visible failure.** An unpaid bill or an overloaded network produces an outage, not just a smaller wallet.
- **Household trade-offs.** A larger home, more occupants or a luxury tier costs more to run.
- **A role for infrastructure.** Utilities create a reason to build, maintain and inspect networks.
- **A balance lever.** Metered consumption is a more realistic way to tune household spending than a single bill.

## What grocery depth adds

- **Stock.** A store can run out, restock and change what is available.
- **Price.** A household can choose cheaper or better food.
- **Spoilage.** Buying in bulk is not free.
- **Nutrition.** Food quality affects health and needs, not just hunger.
- **A shared system.** Players and NPCs buy from the same stores and compete for the same stock.

## What this is not

- It is not a survival game. Needs already exist; utilities and food deepen them, not replace them.
- It is not a player-run utility monopoly in V5a.
- It is not a full supply-chain simulation with farms, imports and logistics.
- It is not a reason to punish poor households with unavoidable death. Arrears, shelters and support systems still exist.

## Design consequence

The smallest useful model is one authoritative network per service and one catalog per store. The economy can be recalibrated around real consumption, and later features can add ownership, markets and logistics without changing the household contract.

## What the player should notice

- The home has bills that reflect how many people live there and how much they use.
- An outage is a visible event with a cause.
- The shop has food, prices and limits.
- A balanced diet is possible but not free.
