# What comes after V5

V5 closes the loop from simulated society to playable life. It does not finish the game.

## Likely V6 directions

- **Deeper economy.** Player-owned businesses, player-run shops, utility ownership, a real grocery supply chain and company competition.
- **Deeper family.** Divorce, custody, adoption, surrogacy, a richer genome and health predispositions.
- **Deeper world.** More building kinds, venues, transport, districts and a larger map.
- **Deeper social.** Voice, groups, events, player-owned venues and moderated community spaces.
- **Deeper client.** A native or Godot client if the browser reaches its limits; character customization; richer animation.
- **Deeper operations.** Multiple worlds, sharding, regional deployment and a live-ops toolchain.

## What should not be assumed

- That the browser client is the final client forever.
- That one world is enough at scale.
- That the current balance bands are the final target.
- That the trait schema will not need a richer model.
- That social features can grow without a moderation team.

## Decision points after V5

1. Is the browser client fast enough on target hardware, or is a native client justified?
2. Is the economy stable enough to add player-owned businesses?
3. Does the social layer need groups and events, or is chat enough?
4. Is the family model ready for divorce, adoption and a richer genome?
5. Does the world need multiple instances or a larger map?

## What to preserve

- Server authority.
- The closed money loop.
- Deterministic replay.
- Admin observability.
- Consent, privacy and moderation.
- The rule that a feature is not done until it has evidence.

## Success signal

V5 leaves a codebase and a set of documents that make the next roadmap a choice, not a rescue.
